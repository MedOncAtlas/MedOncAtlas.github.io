Medical Oncology Algorithms

MedOncAtlas is a learning resource created by a medical oncology learner, for learners. The goal is to make medical oncology treatment decision-making algorithms more organized, accessible, and easier to navigate as we learn and grow in this field together.

The algorithms are not meant to be definitive or static. They reflect an ongoing learning process, and there is always room for improvement. Editable XMind versions are available for download so you can add your own notes, adapt the algorithms to your learning needs, and update them as evidence and clinical practice evolve.

Your feedback is always welcome! If you spot an error, have a suggestion, or think something could be explained or organized better, please feel free to share your thoughts. This is a resource we can continue to build and improve together, and I would love to learn from your feedback and ideas.
